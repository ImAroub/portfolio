Sejal

Evidence-Grounded Multi-Agent Scientific Debate System

Sejal transforms a scientific question or claim into a structured debate between an Advocate and a Skeptic, then evaluates the debate and produces a final judgment grounded in the scientific evidence used.

Explore AI Workflow
01

Overview

Sejal combines Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with a multi-agent workflow to build scientific debates around retrieved research evidence. The Advocate and Skeptic reason from the same evidence base across multiple rounds before the validated debate moves to the final evaluation stage.

The broader platform also includes user accounts, authentication, and saved debate history.

AI Workflow

The AI layer moves from scientific evidence retrieval to a validated multi-agent debate and final evaluation.

01

Evidence Retrieval & Preparation

The user's question or claim is prepared for semantic retrieval. Relevant research is retrieved from academic sources including OpenAlex and PubMed/PMC, then processed through cleaning, deduplication, text extraction, and chunking.

Multilingual embeddings are generated and used with Chroma for semantic retrieval and evidence selection, producing a relevant and diverse evidence base for the debate.

02

Evidence-Grounded Debate

The Advocate and Skeptic receive the same evidence base. Depending on the available evidence, each round response can contain one to three evidence-backed points.

Each point pairs a direct quotation from a scientific paper with an interpretation of what that quotation supports. After presenting the evidence points, the agent summarizes its overall position for the round.

Round response 1–3 Evidence Points Agent Position

Before a response is accepted and displayed, its structure, evidence references, and quotations are validated. If validation fails, the response is corrected and checked again before it can enter the debate.

03

Adaptive Stopping

After the first round, later responses are checked for meaningful novelty before they are added to the debate. Each agent considers the previous discussion and is expected to contribute a new argument rather than restate an earlier point.

If a later response does not add a meaningful new argument, it is not accepted as a new debate contribution. The debate stops and proceeds directly to final evaluation.

04

Evaluation & Final Judgment

The final evaluation receives the retrieved evidence and the complete validated debate. It evaluates the Advocate and Skeptic independently and determines whether one position is better supported or neither is clearly favored.

The scientific claim itself is also assessed separately before a concise final answer is generated, explaining what the evidence supports and any important limitation.

The final answer passes an additional validation step to ensure that it remains consistent with the completed evaluation and does not introduce unsupported scientific conclusions.
