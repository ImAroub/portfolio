Hafidah | حافظة

Invoice & Post-Purchase Management System

Hafidah is an Arabic web application for managing purchase invoices and everything that follows them, including warranties, returns, replacements, renewals, alerts, and post-purchase operations.

View on GitHub

Hafidah landing page introducing the invoice and post-purchase management system.

01

Overview

Hafidah centralizes purchase information in one place so users can organize their invoices, track important periods after a purchase, receive upcoming deadline alerts, and maintain a history of related operations.

02

My Role

I independently developed Hafidah from end to end, including the database structure, backend logic, user interface, authentication, invoice management, alerts, operations, and administrative functionality.

System Overview

Dashboard

The dashboard brings invoices, operations, upcoming alerts, and recent activity together in one place.

Core Functionality

Key Features

Invoice Management

Create, organize, update, search, and filter purchase invoices.

Period Tracking

Track warranty, return, replacement, and renewal periods.

Upcoming Alerts

Surface approaching deadlines before important periods expire.

Operations History

Keep post-purchase operations linked to their original invoices.

Search & Filtering

Find invoices by product, category, status, period, and priority.

User & Admin Management

Support user accounts alongside administrative management tools.

Invoice Management

Organizing Purchase Information

Users can browse their invoices, apply filters, view status information, and open an invoice to see its full post-purchase details.

Invoice list with search, filters, categories, and status indicators.

Invoice details with warranty, return, replacement, subscription, and related operations.

Beyond the Invoice

Post-Purchase Tracking

Hafidah does more than store invoices. Users can record post-purchase events such as maintenance, replacement, return, cancellation, and renewal while keeping each operation connected to the original purchase.

Operations remain connected to the invoice to create a clear post-purchase history.
