Hafidah

Invoice & Post-Purchase Management System

Hafidah is an Arabic web application for managing purchase invoices and everything that follows them, including warranties, returns, replacements, renewals, alerts, and post-purchase operations.

View on GitHub
01

Overview

Hafidah centralizes purchase information in one place so users can organize their invoices, track important periods after a purchase, receive upcoming deadline alerts, and maintain a history of related operations.

02

My Role

I independently developed Hafidah from end to end, including the database structure, backend logic, user interface, authentication, invoice management, alerts, operations, and overall system flow.

System Introduction

Landing Page

The landing page introduces the system and highlights its purpose: helping users keep their invoices and post-purchase details organized in one place.

System Overview

Dashboard

The dashboard brings invoices, operations, recent additions, and upcoming alerts together in one place so users can quickly understand their current status.

Key Features

Invoice Management

Create, organize, update, search, and filter purchase invoices.

Period Tracking

Track warranty, return, replacement, and renewal periods.

Upcoming Alerts

Surface approaching deadlines before important periods expire.

Operations History

Keep post-purchase operations linked to their original invoices.

Search & Filtering

Find invoices by product, category, status, period, and priority.

User Account Management

Support user access, authentication, and organized personal records.

Invoice Management

Managing Purchase Invoices

Users can browse stored purchases, apply filters, and open detailed invoice pages to review product, store, period, and status information.

Invoice List

Search, filtering, categories, and status indicators make stored purchases easier to organize and review.

Invoice Details

Each invoice keeps purchase information together with warranty, return, replacement, and renewal periods.

Post-Purchase Tracking

Managing Post-Purchase Operations

Hafidah keeps post-purchase events linked to the original invoice, allowing users to record maintenance, returns, replacements, renewals, and other related actions.
