Hafidah

Invoice & Post-Purchase Management System

Hafidah is an Arabic web application for managing purchase invoices and the post-purchase lifecycle. It centralizes invoice records, warranty and return periods, subscription renewals, alerts, and related operations within a single system.

View on GitHub

Overview

The system is designed to keep purchase information accessible and organized after purchase. Users can store invoice data and images, monitor time-sensitive periods, receive alerts for approaching deadlines, and maintain a structured history of actions associated with each purchase.

My Role

I independently designed and implemented Hafidah during my cooperative training, covering the relational database structure, PHP backend APIs, SPA interface, authentication, file handling, business rules, invoice and operation workflows, and a separate administration panel.

Landing Page

The landing page introduces Hafidah and communicates the purpose of the system before users enter the application. It presents the relationship between invoice storage, deadline tracking, and post-purchase management in a concise workflow.

Dashboard

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of the user's current records, including invoice and operation totals, recent activity, and upcoming alerts. This allows items requiring attention to be identified without navigating through multiple pages.

Key Features

Invoice Management

Create, update, organize, and maintain purchase records and invoice images.

Deadline Tracking

Automatically calculate and monitor warranty, return, replacement, and renewal expiration dates.

Alerts & Prioritization

Flag deadlines approaching within 14 days and prioritize records that require timely attention.

Operation History

Maintain a chronological record of maintenance, returns, replacements, renewals, and other actions.

Search & Filtering

Search and filter records by category, status, operation type, purchase period, and deadlines, with multiple sorting options.

Administration Panel

Manage users, categories, invoices, and operations through a separate administrative interface.

Invoice Management

Purchase records can be searched, filtered, and prioritized from the invoice view, while each individual record provides access to its purchase details and associated time periods.

Post-Purchase Operations

Each invoice maintains a structured history of events that occur after purchase. Operations can document maintenance, maintenance completion, replacement, return, renewal, cancellation, and other actions, together with their dates, costs, notes, and supporting files.
